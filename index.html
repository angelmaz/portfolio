CheckCells / Seaman PRO — AI-Powered Laboratory Analysis & Hardware Ecosystem

Contributing to the evolution of an optical imaging device, computer vision validation, and laboratory software within a broader ecosystem that also includes a patient-facing AI companion.

End-to-End Product System

Semen analysis traditionally required tedious, error-prone manual microscopic counting. CheckCells built Seaman PRO to automate quantitative parameter detection (Concentration, Motility, Morphology, Vitality, pH) referenced against WHO 6th ed. standards.

Key Hardware & Interaction Iterations

  • Manual Focus Calibration: Replaced static focal distances with a rotatable threaded upper cylinder, guided by live-feed software instructions.
  • Spatial Wayfinding: Redesigned slide rails with precision alignment and etched chamber numbering (1–4) directly onto the hardware.
  • Structural Stability: Shifted from lightweight circular bases to a solid rectangular base to improve stability and reduce unwanted optical shift during sample handling.
  • Human-in-the-Loop AI: Compared model detections against manual reference counts; labeled ~500 images and video frames in Label Studio to support model improvement.
  • Documentation: Co-authored Seaman PRO Instructions for Use (IFU) documentation and lab operator guidance.

MoodBridge 2.0 — Ethical Intelligence-Augmentation System

Designing an emotional regulation companion focused on human autonomy, decision fatigue reduction, and responsible AI safeguards.

Paradigm: Prescriptive Automation vs. Augmented Intelligence

In emotional regulation, highly prescriptive AI systems can increase friction and reduce a user's sense of control. MoodBridge instead uses an Augmented Intelligence model: AI proposes one contextual option, while the user retains full authority to accept, modify, or skip it.

Dimension Prescriptive Automation Intelligence Augmentation (MoodBridge)
Control System determines next step AI proposes → User decides
Engagement Persistent tracking / streaks Optional check-ins / no streak penalties
Data Privacy Cloud-dependent personalization Privacy-first / local-first approach

User Research Insights

  • Decision Fatigue: In low-mood states, choosing from large catalogs of activities increases cognitive burden; the interface must present exactly one realistic micro-action.
  • Guilt Mechanics: Standard gamification and streak counters exacerbate anxiety on missed days; the system must support guilt-free skipping.
  • Tone Sensitivity: Overly cheerful or prescriptive language can reduce trust and engagement; the interaction requires neutral, grounded communication.

TaskApp — Cross-Platform Task Architecture & Usability Engineering

Designing an intuitive task organization system for busy professionals managing split home and work responsibilities.

Research & The Persona (Lukas)

Interviews revealed that busy professionals (like Lukas, a software engineer managing dual work and family logistics) struggle with cluttered apps, lack of category filtering, and poor hands-free entry during commutes.

End-to-End Design Process

  • Paper Wireframing: Sketched 5 iterative layout variants exploring rapid category tagging, calendar placement, and bottom thumb-zone actions.
  • Information Architecture & Flow: Mapped complete screen architecture in Figma connecting tag lists, task modals, date pickers, and category directories.
  • Usability Testing (2 Rounds, 6 Participants):
    • Round 1: Identified strong demand for hands-free voice command entry and shared list coordination.
    • Round 2: Resolved folder selection friction during task creation by making category assignment explicit upon opening the creation modal.

Flying Recipes 2.0 — Cross-System Kitchen Adaptation

Eliminating cognitive friction and mental recalculation for European home cooks in the United States.

The Everyday Systemic Friction

European cooks in the US struggle with dual conversion barriers: translating grams to volumetric cups/ounces, Celsius to Fahrenheit, and recalculating ingredient proportions when moving between standard European and non-standard American baking pan geometries.

Core System Capabilities

  • Quick Unit Conversion: Lightweight real-time weight (g ↔ oz), volume (ml ↔ cups), and temperature (°C ↔ °F) tools.
  • Surface-Area Proportional Scaling: Automatically calculates dimensional area ratios (e.g. 8" round vs 9x13" rectangular pan) and scales ingredient masses instantaneously.
  • Kitchen-Context Usability: High-contrast glanceable ingredient blocks and checkable preparation steps optimized for mobile screens and split attention with messy hands.
  • Full-Stack Architecture: Custom recipe builder and web scraper backed by PostgreSQL, Flask, and JavaScript.
